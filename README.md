## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Mechatronic Systems Engineering student at Simon Fraser University**, completing my Bachelor of Applied Science in December 2026. My interests span **embedded systems, control systems, robotics, mechanical design, and engineering automation**—especially projects that require hardware, software, and mechanical components to work together reliably.

Through engineering co-op placements at **FortisBC** and **PCL Construction**, I have worked in technical environments where documentation, verification, data quality, safety, and cross-disciplinary coordination matter. My academic and personal projects extend that experience into microcontroller development, electromechanical prototyping, CAD, control-system analysis, and tools that streamline engineering workflows.

I approach engineering by breaking complex problems into measurable requirements, developing practical solutions, testing their performance, and documenting the decisions behind them. This GitHub profile brings together selected implementations and technical documentation supporting the work featured on my [engineering portfolio](https://icarus909.com).
